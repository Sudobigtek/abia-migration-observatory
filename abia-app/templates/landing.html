Three Pillars of Protection

🛡️

Prevention

Community awareness programs, school-based safe migration education, and real-time fake job alerts to stop trafficking before it starts.

🏠

Protection

Victim intake, shelter capacity tracking, psychosocial rehabilitation, and family reunification with full case management.

⚖️

Prosecution

Perpetrator database, evidence chain-of-custody with IPFS hashing, court case tracking, and NAPTIP coordination.
